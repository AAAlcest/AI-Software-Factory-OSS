# Public Privacy Standard

Public extraction is deny-by-default.

## Do not sanitize-copy private history

Do not copy private operational documents and merely replace names. Re-author reusable mechanisms from first principles in generic language.

Do not publish:

- private correspondence;
- real tenure/handoff history;
- personal data;
- customer/company-confidential facts;
- credentials, secrets or tokens;
- private runtime topology, hosts, ports, paths or service names;
- real production/release evidence;
- private Git history;
- identifying material that remains sensitive after superficial renaming.

## Allowed pattern

Publish generalized mechanisms, role models, workflow rules, trust-boundary patterns, synthetic correspondence and synthetic examples.

A demo Factory should be entirely fictional and understandable without access to any private Factory.
