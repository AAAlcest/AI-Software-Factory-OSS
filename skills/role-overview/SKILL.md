# Skill: role-overview

Purpose: summarize one Staff Office/role and its active project relationships.

Inputs: logical role ID or Office pointer.

Read: Office README/authority pointer, desk/CURRENT, active pending items and the state pointers for projects currently attached to the role.

Output: role/title, incumbent/lifecycle when configured, responsibility bundle, active projects, pending, reporting/escalation, authority boundaries, source refs and next action.

Cross-project visibility never becomes cross-project execution authority.
