# Roles and Authority

## Roles are logical; chats are physical

A Factory role is a durable organizational identity. One AI conversation may carry several compatible responsibilities, and one logical role may move between models or execution surfaces over time.

Do not create one chat per title merely to imitate an organization chart.

## Recommended small-Factory layout

```text
Human Factory Director
  |
  +-- Primary Management AI
  |     Deputy / Executive
  |     Product + Development Management
  |     Secretary / Records
  |
  +-- Governance / Consistency AI
  +-- Runtime / Infrastructure AI
  +-- Engineering / Project Primary agents
```

## Configurable executive title

The primary management AI may be called:

- Deputy Factory Director;
- Deputy Director / DD;
- VP Product & Engineering;
- VP Engineering;
- COO;
- General Manager;
- Chief of Staff;
- another Human-selected title.

The Factory should govern responsibilities and authority scopes, not depend on the literal title string.

## Authority invariant

```text
technical identity != role identity != authority scope
```

A GitHub account may technically perform actions for several AI roles. Each governed action should still be attributable to the role and authority under which it was performed.
